Lore

Billi started from nothing.

Bybit had been using a cat as part of its brand image for a long time, but this cat didn't have an official name.

That changed on November 17, 2025, when Billi was chosen by the Bybit community from four candidates through a community vote.

This is just the beginning. Billi is a symbol of the connection between TradFi and DeFi on Mantle Chain. At the same time, Billi symbolizes Bybit's evolution beyond its origins as a cryptocurrency exchange into an integrated financial platform that bridges crypto, traditional financial markets, and financial services.

That's why Billi is the mascot of #NewFinancialPlatform.

Who is Billi?

BILLI is a cat that was born on November 17, 2025, after being chosen as the official mascot through a community vote.

The original post announcing BILLI as the official mascot was later deleted due to scam and impersonation issues. However, traces of BILLI can still be found scattered across X posts.

Why Mantle Chain?

Billi stands for the connection between TradFi and DeFi, and Mantle is where that connection actually happens. The token lives there too.

Where do I trade it?

Use a wallet that supports the Mantle network, then swap $BILLI on Oku or Sushiswap. Match the contract address character by character against the one on this page before you swap.

Is this site run by Bybit?

No. This is a community fan page about Billi. For announcements and anything current, go to the official Bybit and Mantle channels directly.
